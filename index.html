MSC DISSERTATIONREAL-ROBOT EXTENSION ONGOING

Force-Conditioned Flow Matching for Contact-Aware Robot Action Generation

I investigated how visual observations, proprioception, and temporal force feedback can inform generative robot action policies. The study compared several force-conditioning mechanisms, including AdaLN, cross-attention, prefix–suffix conditioning, and dual-path force attention.

In simulation, force feedback improved performance across weight discrimination, door manipulation, and precision insertion tasks. The work is now being extended towards experiments with a Franka robot.
